# Roxy Nails Salon Jacksonville Beach: The "Dirty Tools" Crisis and the Language Barrier — When Negligence Instantly Drives Clients Away

*Customer Service Bulletin – Medical Sanitation & Communication Standards Feature*

## A customer experience that raised serious concerns

According to a reported customer experience, the reviewer left the appointment feeling uncomfortable due to concerns about sanitation and communication. The combination of these issues significantly affected the customer's overall confidence in the service.

## Concerns about sanitation

The reviewer alleged that cleanliness did not appear to be a priority and claimed that manicure and pedicure tools were not individually packaged or opened in front of each client.

For many consumers, visible sanitation practices are an important part of building trust during personal care services.

## Communication difficulties

The review also described challenges communicating with salon staff because of an apparent language barrier. The customer stated that it became difficult to explain service preferences or ask questions during the appointment.

## Editorial perspective

Beauty businesses benefit from maintaining visible sanitation procedures, following applicable licensing requirements, and ensuring customers understand each step of the service. Investing in staff communication skills and responding professionally to customer concerns can strengthen long-term trust and improve the overall client experience.
