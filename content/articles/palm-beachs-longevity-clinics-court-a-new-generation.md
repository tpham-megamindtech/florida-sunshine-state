The Florida approach to wellness starts with the light, and Palm Beach shows why. The waiting rooms are getting younger as prevention becomes the point.

That is the backdrop for a change that has been building quietly and is now impossible to ignore. Across Palm Beach and out toward Naples, the same patterns keep surfacing, and the people closest to them have stopped hedging about where things are headed.

"The pieces have been coming together for a while — most people just weren't looking in the right place." says Owen Castellano, a dermatologist who has watched it unfold up close. The details vary from one case to the next, but the throughline is hard to miss.

## Why it matters now

Beneath the calm surface, a real shift is underway. Practitioners in Palm Beach point to a few reasons it is resonating now:

- Community becoming as much a draw as the treatments
- Ingredients and treatments rooted in the local landscape
- Prevention taking priority over quick fixes
- A focus on recovery, not just indulgence

None of it happens by accident. It takes money, timing, and a tolerance for the false starts that come with doing something before it is obvious. What stands out is how many of those ingredients now sit within reach in Palm Beach.

## The bigger picture

Zoom out and this looks less like a single story than a place finding another gear. Devon Sterling, a spa director in Naples, is quick to note that plenty could still go sideways. "What we're seeing now didn't happen overnight, and it isn't going away." But the ambition is real, and so is the momentum behind it.

In Palm Beach, the reset is starting to feel less like a splurge and more like a habit.
