# LT Nails & Spa in Fort Myers: The "Hidden Fees" Crisis and the Over-Filing Disaster — When a $120 Bill Buys Physical Pain

*Customer Service Bulletin – Pricing Transparency & Technical Service Feature*

## A lengthy appointment that ended in disappointment

According to a reported customer experience, the appointment lasted more than two hours and involved multiple technicians before the service was completed. The reviewer stated that the initial work did not meet expectations and that the overall visit became increasingly frustrating.

## Concerns about filing technique

The reviewer alleged that excessive filing caused discomfort during the appointment and believed the natural nails had been over-filed.

## Questions about the final bill

The customer also expressed concern about the final charge, stating that several additional fees appeared on the invoice beyond the expected service price. According to the review, one charge was for a manicure the customer believed had not been performed.

## Editorial perspective

Beauty businesses benefit from transparent pricing, clear communication before adding optional services, and consistent technical standards throughout every appointment.
