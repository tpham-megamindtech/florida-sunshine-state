# Nails by Cathy Bradenton: The "Painful Filing" Crisis and Callousness — When Language Barriers Destroy Revenue

*Customer Service Bulletin – Client Communication & Service Quality Feature*

## A relaxing appointment that became uncomfortable

According to a reported customer experience, a routine pedicure became increasingly uncomfortable because the customer felt the filing process was excessively aggressive. The reviewer believed attempts to communicate discomfort were not understood during the appointment.

## Communication challenges

The customer stated that a language barrier made it difficult to explain that the filing was causing pain. The reviewer also felt that non-verbal signals, including pulling a foot away, were not recognized, resulting in additional discomfort throughout the service.

## Physical discomfort and a changed experience

The reviewer alleged that the rough handling during the appointment caused discomfort extending beyond the feet. Instead of leaving relaxed, the customer reported feeling sore after the visit and no longer viewed the experience as enjoyable.

## Lost business opportunity

The customer explained that a manicure had originally been planned as part of the visit. However, dissatisfaction with the pedicure reportedly led to canceling the additional service before it began.

## Editorial perspective

Clear communication, careful observation of customer comfort, and prompt adjustments during a service are important elements of the salon experience. Paying attention to both verbal and non-verbal feedback may help businesses improve customer satisfaction, encourage repeat visits, and strengthen long-term trust.
